Someone asked me what I ultimately want to achieve with my side projects. I said I hope software makes people's lives easier, and even to my own ears the answer sounded hollow.

After answering, I kept turning the question over, and I realized I don't do side projects out of any sense of purpose. There is no goal I want from all of them as a whole. Instead, each project has its own.

## Each project wants something different

I write the blog to look back on my thinking, write retrospectives, and organize what I have studied.

Pisik, a photo community, and Game Mate, a game service, have a minimum goal of 50 users. I also built the two to revisit technology I had used, [to study infrastructure](/en/blog/why-i-chose-one-ec2-instance-for-my-side-projects), and to write code I could show as a developer. Once they were deployed, though, [the design was disappointing](/en/blog/cognitive-surrender-side-project), the usability was poor, and the features were underwhelming. The quality is too embarrassing to promote, so both are half abandoned for now.

These days I'm building an AI that plays Clash Royale. I had come across [Counterpush](https://app.counterpush.com), a service where you play against an AI, and seeing more AIs on the Clash Royale leaderboard made me want to build one too. I started it partly to study AI, and the goal is first place in the world ranking. Money has nothing to do with it. I'm doing it simply because it interests me.

The studio I'm building alongside it, on the other hand, is for money. It is not a service I will promote to people. The goal is a side job making AI videos such as dramas and animations and uploading them to YouTube. Subscription AI tokens feel like a waste for this, so I plan to run generation on local LLMs.

## Trying to get all three, I stopped at the plan

I had long wanted to do side projects, but the opportunity cost was high, so I only hesitated. Back then I wanted interest, money, and study all at once, and every time I ended up making a plan and stopping there.

## Now one is enough to start

Then models such as Opus 5.5 and GPT-6.1 Sol came out, and the cost became less of a burden than before. Now I can try many things at a low opportunity cost, and I learn while trying them.

So now I start when a project meets just one of the three conditions. If I name the main reason for each, it is study for the blog, Pisik, and Game Mate, interest and study for the Clash Royale AI, and money for the studio. That does not mean the rest is missing. None of them is without interest or without something to learn.

![A table of the goals for the blog, Pisik and Game Mate, the Clash Royale AI, and the studio, with the main reason for starting each given as interest, money, or study](/images/posts/side-project-goals/01-project-goals-en.avif)

I will keep building a service whenever an idea comes up. If no idea came to mind I probably would not build anything, but I tend to have plenty.
